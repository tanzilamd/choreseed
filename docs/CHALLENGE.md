The final article uses that template, omits the optional unshared agent-session section, identifies the real recording separately from the limited free preview, and names Backboard and Browserbase categories based on actual integration evidence.

Entry window: October 19, 2026 at 9:00 AM PDT to October 25, 2026 at 11:59 PM PDT. In Asia/Dhaka: October 19 at 10:00 PM to October 26 at 12:59 PM. Repositories and projects must start and finish within that window. The initial repository commit is October 21, 2026 at 11:23:40 PM Asia/Dhaka, inside the window. Any post-deadline commits must be noted in the README.

One entry per participant per challenge; teams up to four, with handles credited and one team submission. New projects with open-source AI at their core qualify; PRs to existing projects do not. Use the official DEV template and `devchallenge`, `hf26challenge` tags. A published entry needs a demo (deployed link or video), code, explanation of effort saved, and why open innovation matters. This task authorizes an **unpublished** DEV draft only. Draft creation does not enter the contest; public publication is a separate owner action.

Writing Quality is weighted most heavily, followed by relevance, creativity, technical execution and optional partner technology. Partner entries must meaningfully incorporate the technology and identify the category. Applicable categories to assess from actual final delivery: Best Use of Backboard, Best Use of Browserbase, and Best Use of Render. General inference through Backboard is not a guarantee of a partner prize. No Tinker, TabPFN, Paper Instruments or unrelated category is claimed.

Eligibility: at least 18 or the local age of majority, an active DEV member in good standing, valid email/internet access, applicable legal/export restrictions, and no excluded employment/family relationship. The general rules exclude Afghanistan, Belarus, Central African Republic, Cuba, Equatorial Guinea, Iran, Iraq, Kosovo, Libya, Myanmar, North Korea, Russia, South Sudan, Sudan, Syria, Tanzania, Venezuela and Yemen, plus prohibited/sanctioned persons or jurisdictions. Bangladesh is not named in that list; this is not a personal eligibility certification. The owner must satisfy all applicable requirements before entering.

English posts are required for prize judging; AI-assisted development is allowed and should be disclosed. Significant borrowed work must be credited. Optional DevRelay/session sharing is not performed without supported export and privacy review. A submission can win at most one prize per challenge. Neither eligibility nor a prize is guaranteed by this document.
